## Ingredients (3 servings)

- 1kg chicken thighs.
- 3 cloves of garlic.
- 2 large onions.
- 2 carrots.
- 2 celery stalks.
- Parsley root.
- Leek (green part).
- Parsley.
- 2 bay leaves.
- 2l of water.
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice.
- 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns.
- 1 tablespoon vegeta (a popular European vegetable seasoning) or your seasoning mix.

## Procedure

1. Cut the carrot, peeled parsley root and celery stalks into large pieces.
2. Place the chicken pieces into a large pot, cover them with cold water and bring it to a boil over high heat.
3. While waiting for it to boil, cut the onion with peel in half and place it cuted side down in a dry, hot pan without oil and fry without moving it until almost burnt dark brown, almost black.
4. When the water in the pot boils, reduce the heat to medium and carefully remove the grayish foam all the time.
5. When it no longer foams add all the vegetables to the pot including the previously fried onion and peppercorns. Mix it well.
6. Reduce the heat to almost minimum so that there are few bubbles that float to the surface and cook with the pot almost covered for about an hour and a half to two.
7. Then turn off the heat and carefully remove the larger pieces of vegetables and meat from the pot and pick the meat off the bones.
8. Place a thick strainer over a large clean pot which you can also cover it with clean gauze or a kitchen towel that you'll not miss and slowly pour all the soup through this strainer.
9. Put the meat in the strained soup and if you want also boiled vegetables without the onions and discard what you do not use.
10. Add the vegeta to the soup and if you want you can also grounded pepper and some other spices. Mix well and serve.

Bon appetit :).
